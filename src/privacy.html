PortPlay privacy policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

PortPlay shows the picture and sound from a game console connected through a USB HDMI capture dongle. This policy covers the PortPlay website and the PortPlay desktop app, including the version in the Microsoft Store.

What PortPlay collects

Nothing. PortPlay has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your video, sound, screenshots, recordings or settings to anyone, including us.

Camera and microphone

Your computer treats an HDMI capture dongle as a camera for the picture and a microphone for the sound, so PortPlay asks for camera and microphone access. It only uses the capture device you connect, or one you choose in settings. If you choose a microphone to include in recordings, that sound goes into your recordings only. All of this stays on your computer.

Screenshots and recordings

Screenshots, recordings and instant replays are only made when you ask for them. They are saved to your own computer: your Downloads folder on the website, or your Pictures and Videos folders in the desktop app. Screenshots are also copied to your clipboard.

Settings

PortPlay remembers your settings, such as profiles, volume and scaling, in your browser's storage or the app's own storage on your computer. You can clear them at any time by clearing site data in your browser or uninstalling the app.

Downloads and hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the Windows installer is downloaded from GitHub. The Microsoft Store version is provided through the Microsoft Store. Those services may keep standard logs, such as IP addresses, under their own privacy policies. PortPlay itself does not receive that information.

Children

PortPlay does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the project's GitHub issues page.
